# Chair spin and directed rear occlusion v72

The editable Blender scene contains the seated model and camera choreography for 12 seconds at 12 fps, with the upper seat continuously rotating, the floor base stationary, and feet raised for clearance. It retains the v71 layered engine-bay optics and their independent phase schedules. The original v71 project is unchanged.

The final review uses a separate rear-facing compositing pass. It covers actor pixels only where the projected backrest overlaps; it does not change other pixels or front views. This pass is not an automatic arbitrary-angle viewport effect. The included scripts retain the edit/rebuild path. Generated review GIF/MP4 and frames are excluded.
