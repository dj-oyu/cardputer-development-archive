# Chair v65: planted swivel and lightweight V8 source

Editable descendant of v64. The physical model separates a stationary base and four casters from the upper seat. The two near pistons are retained. Six aft pistons' 24 moving parts were removed from the offline engine scene: 6,036 to 4,212 evaluated triangles. The physical asset remains 1,913 human + 370 chair triangles. Retain the v64 revision if the complete offline engine is needed.

The 240×135 design preview uses a fixed world camera, independent piston/liquid/bottle phase tracks, and a ±35-degree swivel. The planted-leg pose, view-relative optics, pin-local spark and restricted inner-metal spill are generated by the separate Python scripts; the .blend is the editable geometric snapshot, not a self-contained recorded turntable or device implementation. Reproducing that preview also needs the v50 physical/portal input, v52 tube source, v54 bubble/slosh textures, and v56 pre-prune optical geometry. Generated frames and clips are omitted. No firmware or device validation is implied.
