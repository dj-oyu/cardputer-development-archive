# Human character — transparent-overlay shape pass

Second art blockout of the existing short-haired human concept proposal,
not a rig or runtime model. Used the exact Space sheet with direction-matched
front, right-facing three-quarter and true side ortho. The concept is an
illustration, not a calibrated turntable; matched views use only per-view
uniform scale/translation and no flattening or mirroring of asymmetric hair.
The overlay informs flatter upper lids, tapering integrated nose, revised
rear bob mass with stacked locks, and a wider planted stance and toe shape.
The concept image is not embedded or included in this distributable work.

Open `human-overlay-02.blend`, or recreate with
`blender -b --python-exit-code 1 --python build_human.py`. The output includes
240×135 whole-character renders and detailed art-only closeups. The 240×135
renders assess readability, not firmware appearance or measured hardware.

Provisional joint centers (X handed, front = −Y): shoulder (±0.465, 0.005,
3.84); elbow (±0.80, −0.02, 3.24); wrist (±1.04, −0.115, 2.60); hip (±0.23,
0.025, 2.80); knee (~±0.38, −0.02, 1.45); ankle (~±0.50, 0.07±0.11, 0.62).
The legs’ fore–aft offset is intentional; verify knee axis by pose and side
view. The torso/hips, sleeves/forearms, boots, and facial elements remain
separate for iteration; there are no skin weights or deformation guarantees.
