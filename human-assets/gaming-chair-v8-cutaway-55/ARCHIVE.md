# Gaming chair v55: 90-degree cutaway V8

This scene replaces the decorative upper engine with a stylized 90-degree V8. Four longitudinal cylinder stations per bank, one crankshaft, four crankpins, and eight moving crowns/rods are separate editable meshes in the cutaway scene. The slotted bores are stationary. The display uses six 60-degree phase bakes for each of the three rear camera angles, clipped to the existing aperture. This is a mechanical visualization, not an engine or combustion simulation.

The physical chair and seated figure inherit v54. The neutral main liquid shadow, independent blue bottle bubble flipbook, and Cycles relit folded hose are retained. The .blend contains authoring geometry and packed proof layers; it does not implement a live Cardputer controller or render the cutaway as geometry on the device. Review videos/images are not external archive artifacts. Reproduction scripts use v54 intermediate assets and the v55 packed images are available for extraction without those intermediates.
