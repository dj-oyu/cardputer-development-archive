# Pipe Dream — open instrument aisle

The near machines and camera framing are retained from the eye-level stage. Left and right stations repeat every 8.55 scene units. Their copper and enamel pipes, brass outlets, bars, and tuned drums diminish by perspective and fade into a low-density Cycles volume, not into an end wall. No character, back wall, rear window, or ceiling spans the aisle.

Open `pipe-dream-open-hall.blend` and render with Cycles to obtain a flat 240×135 opaque RGB backdrop. Rebuild the editable scene and render with `blender -b --python-exit-code 1 --python build_pipe_hall.py`. Add `-- --draft` to inspect a faster local render.
