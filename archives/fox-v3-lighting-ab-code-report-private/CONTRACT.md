They therefore bundle a documented material/ambient meaning change, not merely adding two lights.

No shadow/occlusion/GI/ground emission/specular/AgX is added. HDR is accumulated in local doubles, then clamped once before565; no HDR snapshot/profile exists. Raster remains screen-space vertex565 Gouraud, not pixel physical shading.
