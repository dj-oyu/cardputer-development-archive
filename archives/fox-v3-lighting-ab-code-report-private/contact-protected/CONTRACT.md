# One source-only hard-lock contact reduction

Immutable original blend and baseline GLB hashes are as recorded in prior fox-v3-occlusion-reduction/build-report.json. Only Muzzle pad ±1 and Smile ±1 may change. Old ladder and strict gate remain intact.

Protection is determined once from source pair geometry only. For each original triangle, compute nearest distance to the opposite same-side surface at all three vertices and its centroid. Protect the triangle if the minimum of these four distances is no greater than that triangle's maximum edge length. Expand by one original edge-adjacent face ring. Freeze every protected face's source triangle identity, vertex coordinates, and original corner normals. Freeze all vertices incident to those faces. No images, pixel coordinates, ray views or candidate results influence protection.

One candidate, per-object target pad256 and Smile160. Repeatedly select the shortest eligible edge, stable original-vertex-index tie break, both endpoints unlocked. Collapse higher original vertex ID to lower ID's unchanged source position. Accept only manifold link condition, no surviving degenerate face, no duplicate surviving face, and strictly positive old/new face-normal dot product (no reversal). Recompute eligibility after every accepted collapse. Stop at target or when no legal unlocked edge remains; never remove protected faces or unlock vertices to attain a target. Endpoint collapse introduces no manually displaced vertices. A target may be impossible; report actual cost and failure honestly.

Verify protected original triangle IDs remain, with exact coordinates and original corner normals regardless of compact reindexing. Unprotected normals are recomputed area-weighted on the final geometry; do not claim all normals are preserved. Existing source material assignments and sidedness remain exact. Unrelated original binary data and JSON values remain exact by append-only four-primitive replacement. Original files are never saved or overwritten.

Report initial protected face/vertex rates, legal collapse count, target reached/not reached, remaining unprotected faces, exported POSITION uniqueness and normal splits, resource costs, and preservation guards. Existing source/candidate containment and development ray gates are unchanged. Prior holdout angles are now called seen validation. New unused validation angles are azimuth 7.5+30*k (k=0..11), elevations -22.5,7.5,37.5 degrees, same sample rays/origin scale. Freeze numeric views before evaluating candidate. These views do not choose protection or rerank candidates. This is one method and one candidate; on failure stop rather than tune or add variants.

Review clarifications: The protection width is a conservative source-tessellation-dependent rule, not a guarantee of all-view occlusion. Local edge-link and face-reversal tests do not guarantee absence of nonlocal self-intersection. Final closedness, winding/volume/topology checks and unchanged strict inside/outside gate are mandatory.
